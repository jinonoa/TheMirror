About
Often, when a customer visits a hairdressing salon, she either knows in advance what style she wants, or she chooses a style from the hairdresser’s catalogue. Usually, the cut doesn’t turn out well because it doesn’t suit the customer. Consequently, the customer is dissatisfied, and so is the hairdresser, as she feels she has wasted time creating that particular style. This is where my app comes in. TheMirror will help a customer decide on a hairstyle, satisfy both parties and save time.

Functionality
- Adding, editing or deleting a template from the catalogue
- Model selected by the customer
- Pan shot of the customer's head
- Whether or not the chosen model is appropriate
